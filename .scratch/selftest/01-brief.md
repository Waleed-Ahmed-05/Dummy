# Brief (SYNTHETIC SELF-TEST): StudyBuddy flashcards

**Proposal:** A web app where university students make flashcard decks and review them with spaced repetition. Sign-up with email. Jurisdiction: Pakistan.

**Customer:** first-year university students who cram before exams.
**Objective:** 30% of sign-ups review on 3+ separate days within 2 weeks, by 2026-12-01.
**Hypothesis:** students who get a daily "5 cards due" reminder will return more than those who don't.

## Segments (confirmed by the user)
1. First-year undergrads cramming for exams
2. Tutors who prepare decks for their students

## Planned data
- Email address and display name at sign-up
- Decks and review history
- Planned public claim on the landing page: "Students using StudyBuddy improve their grades by 40%."
